- mosqito/sq_metrics/loudness/loudness_zwtv/_nonlinear_decay.py
- mosqito/sq_metrics/loudness/loudness_zwst/_calc_slopes.py

Modifications, 2026-10-24:
- Scalarized the nonlinear-decay recurrence and specific-loudness slope
  algorithm for Numba JIT execution.
- Retained upstream constants, calculation and rounding order, temporal
  interpolation, threshold rules, and initial-state behavior.
- Added wrappers for gain-scaled cached third-octave power with the
  upstream fixed numerical floor restored after gain application.
- No fast-math, coefficient retuning, new weighting, or alternate
  psychoacoustic model is used.

Other psychoacoustic stages execute the pinned MoSQITo implementation.
Accelerated kernels were compared directly with untouched public API
outputs, including the official test-10 pulse fixture. These comparison
results do not constitute exhaustive ISO conformance certification.
